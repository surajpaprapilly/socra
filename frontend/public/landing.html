Built for the exam room, not the submission.

We ask. You think.

Socra never gives you arguments, outlines, or model answers. Every idea in your Blueprint came from you — which means every idea will still be there when you sit the exam alone.

Rigour over reassurance.

If your argument drifts from the question, Socra will catch it. If your example lacks an analytical link, you will be asked to make one. This is uncomfortable by design.

Transfer, not dependence.

The goal of every session is that Socra's questions become your own internal voice. Six months of sessions should mean you no longer need them.

Four phases. One session. Exam-ready thinking.

Phase 01

Question Autopsy

Before any argument, every loaded term in the question is interrogated and defined. You cannot drift from a question you have properly understood.

Phase 02

Argument Construction

Build three paragraph arguments through dialogue. Each one follows the structure Cambridge examiners reward — topic sentence, development, example, analytical link back to the question.

Phase 03

Stress Test

Your weakest argument gets challenged hard. Not to break you — to find the gaps before the examiner does.

Phase 04

Blueprint

A structured, downloadable essay blueprint built entirely from your own thinking. Every field filled by you, organised by Socra.
